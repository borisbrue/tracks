A live instrument for techno.

tracks. plays whole tracks, loops, a drum rack and a synth on one clock, and you play it from the hardware on your table: Ableton Push 1 for clips and notes, an Allen & Heath Xone:K2 as the mixer. You build a set the way a DJ would and change it the way a live act does.

Launch on the beat, not near it

The first press picks a slot, the second one starts it on the next bar or beat, whatever the launch quantum says. Two separate presses rather than a double click: only that way does the start land exactly where you mean it.

  • Eight hot cues and eight saved loops per clip, live loops from a quarter beat to eight bars, beat jumps that keep the phase.
  • Capture a running loop into a new slot, rearrange the grid by dragging, take back clip edits with Cmd+Z.
  • Space runs the transport, Enter launches the picked clip.

Your library, in time and in key

Every track is analysed once: tempo, beat grid, downbeat, transients, key in Camelot notation and an energy curve. Then it plays in time with everything else, repitched, beat-sliced or stretched.

  • Next suggests what fits after the playing track: matching key, close tempo, and a step down, level or up in energy.
  • Crates, grouping by genre, artist, key or tempo, and a player that prelistens on the cue output.
  • Stems: split a track into drums, bass, other and vocals (on the desktop), and switch them per channel while it plays.

Drums, notes and a synth of its own

A channel can carry a drum rack, a sampler or drift, the synth that comes with tracks. Patterns and melodies are clips like any other: they launch, loop and stop on the same grid.

Effects you hold, not set

Hold a pad and the channel goes into an echo, a wash of reverb or a roll. Let go and everything is back where it was. The same effects sit on the touchscreen, on the Push pads and on the K2's keys, so one hand can end a track in an echo while the other starts the next one.

Clips shows the whole grid; Mix shows the eight strips with EQ, sends and a master column; FX shows the pads. The channel headers mark which effects are on, so you can see what the hardware is doing.

Made for the hardware on the table

The screen is optional while you play. Everything the app does can be done from the controllers, and they all see the same selection.

Ableton Push 1

The session grid with clip colours, launch and stop; hot cues and loops; drum pads, keys, chords and melody steps; the arpeggiator; the FX pads; browsing and loading the library. The display shows parameters and clip names.

Allen & Heath Xone:K2

A mixer that never changes under your hands: EQ with kills, the DJ filter on the encoders, faders, cue, play and an echo per channel, mapped from measured MIDI numbers. Its sound card carries master and cue.

Any MIDI controller

Mappings are short text files: knobs, keys, encoders and whole pad grids in one line each. Learn mode and an editor in the settings build them without typing. Starting points for Launchpad Mini MK3, APC40 and APC Key 25 are included.

Sync with other gear and apps through Ableton Link or MIDI clock, in and out.

One language for everything

Under the screen is a real-time engine in C++20 that never allocates or waits on the audio thread. On top of it sits a small text language. The screen, the Push, the K2 and saved sessions all speak it, so everything that can be done can be typed, mapped and saved.

A session is a script of these lines, and a reloaded session comes back exactly as it was saved. The engine is covered by a couple of hundred tests, including allocation checks on the audio path.

# a channel with a drum rack and a pattern
rack 1 demo hard
pattern 1 1 16
steps 1 1 1 x...x...x...x...

# a track that knows its key, and a synth in it
load 2 1 "Peak Time.wav" auto
drift 3 new reese
key 3 from 2

# launch on the next bar, hold an echo
launch 1 1 4
fxhold 2 echo on
fxhold 2 echo off

Not released yet

tracks. is in active development. There is no download or public build yet. The iPad version is prepared but not shipped.

  • Runs on macOS and Linux; target is the M1 iPad Pro.
  • Built with JUCE and Ableton Link; license to be decided.
  • Controllers: Push 1 and Xone:K2 first, others through mappings.
