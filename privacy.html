Privacy

For the tracks. app on iPad and on the desktop. Last updated 27 November 2026.

Nothing is collected

tracks. has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. It does not send anything about you, your music or how you use it to us or to anyone else. There is no server it talks to.

Your files stay on your device

The tracks you add, the analysis of them (tempo, key, beat grid, energy), your sessions, crates, ratings, presets and controller mappings are stored on your device only - on the iPad in the app's own storage (your music in its Documents folder, which you see in the Files app). They leave the device only when you copy them yourself.

Network

  • On the iPad, tracks. makes no network connections.
  • On the desktop, two optional features use your own machine or local network: Ableton Link, to share tempo with other apps nearby, and External control, a local socket for scripts or an AI assistant you run yourself. External control is off until you switch it on. Neither sends anything to the internet.

MIDI and audio

tracks. talks to the MIDI controllers and audio interfaces you connect. It does not use the microphone.

Questions

tracks. is an independent project by borisbrue. Questions about privacy are welcome as an issue on GitHub.
